Recruitment of participants for a study
=======================================

1. Log in to Castellum by using your username and password
2. Click on **Recruitment** 2.1 If you see the message **There are
   currently no studies you can recruit for**, please follow instruction
   2.2. If you see the relevant study, go to step 3. 2.2 Contact the
   responsible person who has access to the role distribution of the
   study you wish to recruit for

.. note::
   Wäre es sinnvoll, an dieser Stelle einen
   Button **Zugriffsanfrage** o.ä. einzubauen? So könnte eine
   Zugriffsanfrage zur Studie direkt an den/die Verantwortliche/n gesendet
   werden.

3. Click on **Find subjects** next to the study you want to recruit
   participants for
4. By clicking on **Add**, relevant probands will be shown to you
5. Press the **Call** button to contact the person you want to reach

   -  **E-Mail address**, **Phone number**, a potential **Phone number
      alternative** and the **Address** will be shown to you
   -  You might also see the **Preferred contact method**, if the person
      is **currently available** and some **General recruiting notes**
   -  Please have a look at the **Talking points** that the study
      coordinator has defined

.. note::
   Organisatorischer Punkt: was genau soll der Studienanleger hier
   beschreiben? Studienablauf, Abfrage von wichtigen Attributen und/oder
   Anwendung von Methoden in der Studie?

6. CALL/CONTACT

.. note:: Kann man direkt in Castellum die Personen anrufen?

7. Select a **Status of participation request**

   -  Choose between:

   1. **Not reached**
   2. **Unsuitable**

      -  This may apply if attributes of the person do not comply with
         the requirements of the study

   3. **Invited** or
   4. **Recall appointed**

      -  Determine the date and time of the recall appointment

   -  When you select the **Status of participant request** and click on
      **Save and show next**, the next contact person will be shown to
      you automatically

Editing contact data and attributes; adding probands to the study
-----------------------------------------------------------------

.. note::
   Mit **Edit subject** ist doch gemeint, dass die Person als
   Proband/-in zur Studie hinzugefügt und eingeladen wird, richtig? Aus
   Verständlichkeitsgründen lieber **Edit subject to
   study** schreiben?

-  Click on **Edit contact data** in order to do so and press **Save**
-  By clicking **Edit attributes**, you can change characteristics of the
   person that became known in the conversation
-  When you press **Edit subject**, you can determine the **Privacy level**
-  Settle the time-based **Availability** of the person
-  Press **Save** to record the progress
