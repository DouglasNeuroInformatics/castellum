Deleting a subject from Castellum
====================================

Prerequisite
---------------
The subject data of a subject should be completely deleted because of five
different possibilities:

- the subject either requested it,
- it no longer makes sense to keep him or her in the database,
- he or she cannot be reached due to incorrect or missing contact data,
- there is no consent or
- the study in which he or she participated has expired (about five years ago).
You can find the mentioned possibilities for the deletion in the tab
**data protection**.

In order to delete the externally and internally stored data of a test
person, please proceed as follows:
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Log in to Castellum by using your username and password
2. Click on **Data protection** on the front page

- If the data of a proband should be deleted, you will find the proband
assigned to one or more deletion options
3. Click on **Details** next to the subject to be deleted
4. Click on **Delete**

- There will be a message, saying **Are you sure you want to permanently delete
this subject and all related data?**
5. Press **Confirm**

- The subject is now deleted
