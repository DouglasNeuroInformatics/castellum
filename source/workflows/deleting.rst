Deleting a subject from Castellum
====================================

Prerequisite
---------------
The subject data of a subject should be completely deleted because of four
different reasons:

    - the subject either requested it,
    - it no longer makes sense to keep them in the database,
    - they cannot be reached due to incorrect or missing contact data or
    - there is no consent.
You can find the mentioned reasons for the deletion in **data protection** on
the front page.

Deletion of externally and internally stored data
--------------------------------------------------
In order to delete the externally and internally stored data of a subject,
please proceed as follows:
1. Log in to Castellum by using your username and password
2. Click on **Data protection** on the front page

    - If the data of a subject should be deleted, you will find the subject
    assigned to one or more deletion options
3. Click on **Details** next to the subject to be deleted
4. Click on **Delete**

    - If you see a message saying **Are you sure you want to permanently delete
    this subject and all related data?**,
    - Press **Confirm** and the subject will be deleted

    - If you see a message saying **This subject cannot be deleted because
    there still is data about them in studies.**, proceed as follows:

5. Go to the responsible person of the study
    
    - Ask the responsible person to delete all data of the subject you want
    to delete
    - You can find the responsible person by clicking on **Study
    Participation**, then press **Contact** next to the person to be deleted
    
    

Deleting a study from Castellum
====================================
    
Prerequisite
---------------
A study should be completely deleted because of one or more of thefollowing
reasons:
    - The study is no longer current because it was completed five years
    or more ago
    - ....
    
Deleting the study
-------------------
1. Go to **Studies** on the front page
2. Click on **All studies** to see all proposed studies
3. Choose the stufy you want to delete by pressing **Details** next to it
